Article on cultivating purpose in life. Key points:

**What is purpose?** Having a significant reason beyond the immediate for how and why you do things.

**The Passion Test** (Chris and Janet Attwood):
1. Write "When my life is ideal, I am..." 15 times with different endings
2. Compare pairs and eliminate until you have top 5

**Benefits of purpose** (from research):
- Fewer chronic conditions
- Greater emotional resilience
- Better sleep
- Lower rates of premature mortality

**Key questions to ask:**
- What is most meaningful to you?
- What would you do regardless of reward or recognition?
- What would you make sacrifices for?

"The mystery of human existence lies not in just staying alive, but in finding something to live for" — Fyodor Dostoyevsky
