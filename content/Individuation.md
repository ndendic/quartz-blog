# Individuation

**Jung's core process of becoming whole by integrating unconscious aspects of the psyche into consciousness.**

## Overview
Individuation is the central goal of Jungian psychology — a lifelong journey toward psychological wholeness. It requires confronting and integrating the Shadow, encountering the Anima/Animus, and ultimately realizing the Self as the organizing center of the total personality. It is not about perfection or isolation, but about becoming fully and authentically oneself.

## Key Aspects
- Lifelong process, not a destination
- Requires Shadow integration as its first major task
- Involves encounter with the Anima (in men) or Animus (in women)
- Culminates in Self realization — the archetype of wholeness
- Not individualism — it deepens connection to others and the collective
