# Ryan Holiday

**American author, modern Stoicism popularizer**

## Key Ideas
- **The Obstacle is the Way** — obstacles don't block the path, they are the path; adversity creates growth
- **Ego is the Enemy** — ego sabotages learning, relationships, and long-term success at every stage
- **Stoicism as practice** — ancient Stoic philosophy (Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus) applied to modern life
- **Daily Stoic** — short daily practices and reflections to embody Stoic principles
