# Stevan Pešić

**Serbian playwright** — author of the Tesla interview play

## Key Ideas
- **Tesla or the Adjustment of the Angels** (*Tesla ili prilagođavanje anđela*) — dramatized imaginary interview with [[Nikola Tesla]] exploring his philosophy, spirituality, and inner life
- Frames Tesla not just as inventor but as mystic and philosopher
- Gives literary voice to Tesla's ideas on rhythm, light, and the unity of science and spirit
