# Archetypes

**Universal symbolic patterns in the collective unconscious that shape human experience across all cultures.**

## Overview
Archetypes are primordial images and patterns inherited by all humans, not personally acquired. They are not fixed personalities but dynamic organizing principles that manifest differently depending on culture, individual, and context. They appear consistently in mythology, religion, dreams, and art worldwide — evidence of a shared psychic substrate.

## Key Aspects
- Core archetypes: Hero, Shadow, Anima/Animus, Wise Old Man/Woman, Trickster, Self, Great Mother
- Not fixed images but dynamic patterns that take culturally specific forms
- Appear across unconnected cultures in myths, dreams, and art
- The Self is the central archetype — the totality and organizing principle of the psyche
- Activated during key life transitions and psychological crises
