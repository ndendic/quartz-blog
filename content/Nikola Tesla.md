# Nikola Tesla

**Serbian-American inventor, electrical engineer, visionary** (1856–1943)

## Key Ideas
- **Alternating current & wireless energy** — transformed how civilization transmits and uses power
- **Science-spirituality unity** — believed science and spirit were two paths to the same truth
- **Energy, frequency, vibration** — "If you want to find the secrets of the universe, think in terms of energy, frequency and vibration"
- **Life is rhythm** — viewed all existence through cyclical, rhythmic patterns
