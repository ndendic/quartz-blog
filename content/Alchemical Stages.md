# Alchemical Stages

**Jung's mapping of psychological transformation onto the four stages of alchemical transmutation.**

## Overview
Jung spent decades studying alchemy and concluded that alchemists were unconsciously projecting psychological transformation onto matter. The four stages of the alchemical opus — nigredo, albedo, citrinitas, rubedo — map precisely onto the stages of individuation: from confronting darkness, through purification and illumination, to final integration and wholeness. The "philosopher's stone" is a symbol for the individuated Self.

## Key Aspects
- **Nigredo** (blackening) — confronting the Shadow; dark night of the soul; dissolution of the old self
- **Albedo** (whitening) — purification; withdrawal and reflection; gaining clarity after the darkness
- **Citrinitas** (yellowing) — awakening of solar consciousness; emerging insight; dawn after winter
- **Rubedo** (reddening) — full integration; the philosopher's stone; wholeness achieved
- Seasonal mapping used in the Individua app: Winter=Nigredo, Spring=Albedo, Summer=Citrinitas, Autumn=Rubedo
