# Deep Life Framework

This folder contains the Deep Life meta-framework based on Cal Newport's Deep Life Stack 2.0.

## Contents
- 8 Stage Plans (Discipline, Control, Craft, etc.)
- System guides and workflows
- Deep Life MOC

## Purpose
Deep Life is the **lens** through which projects are evaluated, not just a storage location. Every project should connect back to one of the 8 stages.
