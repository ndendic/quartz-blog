# Deep Life Stack 2.0 - [[Cal Newport]]


Episode 274 of the Deep Questions podcast.

## 2 Stage Process

### Stage 1: Become a Capable Human

1. **Discipline** - in 3 areas:
   - **Body** - health, exercise, eating, sleep
   - **Mind** - reading, intellectual pursuits, meditation
   - **Heart** - relationships, call friends/family, time with kids
   - *Obsidian: daily tasks using Tasks plugin and daily notes*

2. **Control** - Organization system
   - *Obsidian: figure out organization system*

3. **Craft** - Learn to do something well
   - *Obsidian: projects with plans*

4. **Simplification** - Remove what's not important
   - *Simplify Obsidian management, add AI agents*

### Stage 2: Cultivate Depth

5. **Values** - One note to outline them all
6. **Service** - Outline the plan
7. **Transformation** - Reflection notes and plans
8. **Legacy** - Reflection notes and plans
