# Walter Russell

**American polymath — painter, sculptor, architect, philosopher** (1871–1963)

## Key Ideas
- **The Universal One** — all matter and energy are expressions of one undivided light/mind
- **Rhythmic Balanced Interchange** — the universe operates through balanced cycles of giving and receiving
- **Light/Mind cosmogony** — consciousness precedes and creates physical reality
- **Beauty, Truth, Goodness triad** — the three qualities of the Universal Mind
