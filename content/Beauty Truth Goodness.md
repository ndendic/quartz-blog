# Beauty, Truth, Goodness

**The classical triad of transcendental values — the foundation of Nikola's creative philosophy.**

> "Create beauty in alignment with truth and goodness."

## Overview

The triad of Beauty, Truth, and Goodness traces from Plato through Western philosophy to [[Walter Russell]], who grounded it in his cosmogony of Light and Mind. In this vault, it serves as the guiding principle for all creative and professional work — code, writing, relationships, and art should aspire to all three.

## The Three Pillars

- **Beauty** — aesthetic excellence, harmony, proportion. Not decoration but deep structural elegance.
- **Truth** — honesty, accuracy, alignment with reality. Saying what is, building what works.
- **Goodness** — ethical action, service, benefit to others. Creating value that helps.
