# Neuroplasticity - Huberman Lab

## Key Framework: A-F
- **A - Alertness**: Emotional attachment triggers learning
- **F - Focus**: Direct your sight to focus attention

## Key Concepts
- NSDR (Non-Sleep Deep Rest) for knowledge comprehension and energy recovery
- Brain changes happen through:
  - Acetylcholine (spotlight attention)
  - Adrenaline (alertness)
  - Dopamine (reward/motivation)

## Important Points
- Plasticity changes significantly after age 25
- Competition is the route to plasticity
- Agitation during learning is key
- Ultradian focus cycles (~90 minutes)
- Real change occurs during rest/sleep after learning
