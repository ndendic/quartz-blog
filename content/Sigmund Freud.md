# Sigmund Freud

**Austrian neurologist, founder of psychoanalysis** (1856–1939)

## Key Ideas
- **The Unconscious** — most mental life occurs below conscious awareness and drives behavior
- **Dream interpretation** — dreams are the "royal road" to the unconscious; symbols mask repressed wishes
- **Id, Ego, Superego** — structural model of the psyche: instinct, rational mediator, internalized morality
- **Psychosexual development** — early childhood experiences shape adult personality and neurosis
