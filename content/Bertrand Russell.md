# Bertrand Russell

**British philosopher, logician, Nobel laureate** (1872–1970)

## Key Ideas
- **Analytical philosophy** — clarity of language and logical rigor as the foundation of good thinking
- **The Conquest of Happiness** — happiness comes from engagement with the world, not self-absorption; cultivate zest
- **Foundations of mathematics** — with Whitehead wrote *Principia Mathematica*, grounding math in formal logic
- **Skeptical empiricism** — beliefs should be proportioned to evidence; question authority including your own assumptions
