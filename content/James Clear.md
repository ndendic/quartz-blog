# James Clear

**American author and habits expert** — popularized the science of tiny habits

## Key Ideas
- **Atomic Habits** — small 1% improvements compound dramatically over time
- **Identity-based habits** — lasting change comes from shifting who you believe you are, not just what you do
- **Habit stacking** — attach new habits to existing ones to leverage established neural pathways
- **Systems over goals** — goals set direction, but systems determine whether you actually get there
