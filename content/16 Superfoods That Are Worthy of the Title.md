Kale is considered one of the most nutrient-dense vegetables on the planet due to its many vitamins, minerals and antioxidants. It contains 684% DV vitamin K, 206% DV vitamin A, and 134% DV vitamin C per cup.

Key superfoods covered:
- Kale - rich in vitamins A, C, K
- Microgreens - up to 40x more nutrients than mature plants
- Collard greens - excellent vitamin K source
- Spinach - high in folate
- Cabbage - cancer-protective glucosinolates
- Beet greens - rich in potassium and antioxidants
- Watercress - potential cancer-fighting properties
- Romaine lettuce - vitamins A and K
- Swiss chard - contains syringic acid for blood sugar
- Arugula - natural nitrates for blood flow
- Endive - contains kaempferol antioxidant
- Bok choy - selenium for thyroid health
- Turnip greens - stress-reducing antioxidants
