# Flow

**A state of optimal experience — complete absorption in a challenging activity that matches one's skill level.**

## Overview
Flow was named and studied by psychologist Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi. It occurs when a task's challenge level is high enough to fully engage you but not so high it triggers anxiety — and your skill level matches. In flow, self-consciousness dissolves, time distorts, and the activity becomes intrinsically rewarding. It can be cultivated deliberately through environment design and skill development.

## Key Aspects
- Coined by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi based on decades of research
- Requires challenge-skill balance — too easy causes boredom, too hard causes anxiety
- Characterized by loss of self-consciousness and distorted time perception
- Intrinsically rewarding — the activity itself becomes the reward
- Can be cultivated through deliberate practice and distraction-free environments
