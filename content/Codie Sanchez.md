# Codie Sanchez

**American entrepreneur, investor, founder of Contrarian Thinking** — advocate for buying boring businesses

## Key Ideas
- **Boring businesses** — laundromats, car washes, service businesses generate reliable cash flow with less competition
- **Buying vs building** — acquiring existing profitable businesses is faster and lower-risk than starting from scratch
- **Main Street investing** — small local businesses are an underrated asset class accessible to ordinary people
- **Cash-flow first** — prioritize businesses that pay from day one over growth-only plays
