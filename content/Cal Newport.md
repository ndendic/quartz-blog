# Cal Newport

**American author, computer science professor at Georgetown** — architect of the Deep Work and Deep Life frameworks

## Key Ideas
- **Deep Work** — focused, distraction-free work on cognitively demanding tasks produces outsized results
- **Deep Life framework** — 8-stage progression from discipline and control through to legacy and mission
- **Time Block Planning** — scheduling every hour intentionally rather than reacting to demands
- **Digital Minimalism** — curating technology use around genuine values, not defaults
