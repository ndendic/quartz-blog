# Carl Jung

**Swiss psychiatrist, founder of analytical psychology** (1875–1961)

## Key Ideas
- **Individuation** — the lifelong process of integrating unconscious content to become a whole self
- **Shadow** — the repressed or unknown aspects of the psyche that must be confronted, not denied
- **Archetypes & Collective Unconscious** — universal patterns and symbols shared across humanity
- **Active Imagination & Synchronicity** — methods for engaging the unconscious; meaningful coincidences as psychic events
