# Naval Ravikant

**Indian-American entrepreneur, investor, philosopher of wealth creation** (b. 1974)

## Key Ideas
- **Specific Knowledge** — knowledge that cannot be trained, only earned through genuine curiosity and experience
- **Leverage** — code, media, capital, and labor multiply your efforts; code and media are permissionless
- **Accountability** — taking personal risk under your own name is how you build reputation and wealth
- **Happiness as a skill** — contentment is something you practice, not something that happens to you
