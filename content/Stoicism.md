# Stoicism

**Ancient Greco-Roman philosophy of virtue, rational self-mastery, and resilience.**

## Overview

Founded by Zeno of Citium (~300 BCE), Stoicism teaches that virtue (wisdom, courage, justice, temperance) is the highest good. External events are indifferent — what matters is our response. Key Stoic thinkers include Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, and Seneca. Modern popularization through [[Ryan Holiday]].

## Core Principles

- **Dichotomy of Control** — focus only on what you can control (your judgments, actions, will)
- **Virtue as the Sole Good** — character over circumstance
- **Amor Fati** — love of fate, embracing everything that happens
- **Memento Mori** — remember death to live fully
- **Premeditatio Malorum** — negative visualization to build resilience
