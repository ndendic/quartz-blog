## Does Karl Friston's neuroscience point to non-dualism?

Seeing | Neuroscience | 2026-03-27

![A digital rendering of a human brain showcasing neural pathways and connections as a complex network of light.](https://www.essentiafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/shutterstock_2555518845-scaled-2000x1000.jpg)

Prof. Karl Friston is the most cited neuroscientist in the world, renowned for pioneering the framework of active inference and for developing the influential Free Energy Principle (FEP). In this conversation with Hans Busstra, Friston touches on the metaphysics of his FEP, which surprisingly points to non-dualism instead of materialism, as widely assumed. According to Friston, space and time, the self, and even the FEP itself are mere stories, useful fictions with real explanatory power, which do not give us direct access to the noumenal reality.

## Connections

### Related vault notes

- [[Carl Jung]] — Friston's notion that the self is a "useful fiction" and a model the brain constructs resonates with Jung's idea that the ego is only one complex among many within a larger psychic totality. Both point to the self as an emergent boundary, not a fundamental substance.

- [[Synchronicity]] — Friston's Markov blankets define boundaries between systems that nonetheless remain statistically coupled. This offers a formal mechanism for how seemingly separate systems could exhibit meaningful correlations — the very pattern Jung described as synchronicity.

- [[Walter Russell]] — Russell's cosmology posits that all matter is a projection of mind, with apparent duality arising from a deeper unity. Friston arrives at a strikingly similar conclusion from neuroscience: space, time, and selfhood are inferential constructs, not features of noumenal reality.

- [[Jung and Technology]] — Friston's insight that even the Free Energy Principle is "itself a story" raises the question of whether AI systems that implement predictive coding are generating their own useful fictions. Relevant to the blog draft's exploration of Jungian archetypes in technological systems.

- [[Convergence Map]] — Key convergence point: neuroscience (Friston) arriving at non-dualism independently of contemplative traditions. The FEP provides a mathematical bridge between empirical brain science and perennial philosophy.

### Related clippings (Essentia Foundation series)

- [[Integrated Information Theory explained]] — IIT and FEP are rival but complementary theories of consciousness. Friston himself addresses the comparison at 1:13:15, noting that IIT focuses on being (intrinsic information) while FEP focuses on doing (active inference). Together they bracket the hard problem from two directions.

- [[Spacetime is the memory of a self knowing universe]] — Faggin's claim that spacetime is the "permanent memory" of cosmic self-knowing maps directly onto Friston's idea that our generative models create pasts and futures as inferential constructs. Both deny that time is a fundamental feature of reality.
