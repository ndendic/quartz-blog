# Shadow

**The repressed, unconscious aspect of personality containing everything the ego refuses to identify with.**

## Overview
The Shadow is the first major figure encountered in Jungian individuation. It holds the traits, impulses, and memories we have rejected or hidden — often formed in childhood through social conditioning. Critically, the Shadow contains not only dark material but also "golden shadow": positive qualities we've suppressed, such as creativity, assertiveness, or joy. Integration, not elimination, is the goal.

## Key Aspects
- Contains both negative traits and suppressed positive potential ("gold in the shadow")
- Must be acknowledged and integrated — not defeated or bypassed
- Shadow work is the entry point and first stage of individuation
- Unintegrated Shadow is projected onto others (we see in them what we won't see in ourselves)
- Appears in dreams as a same-sex figure or antagonist
